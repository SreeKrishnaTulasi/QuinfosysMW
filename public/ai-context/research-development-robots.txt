# Quinfosys Research and Development Page Context

Page: Research and Development
Route: /research-development
Purpose: Content oriented knowledge page for Quinfosys research areas, technologies, applied projects, and collaboration pathways.

Primary intent:
The Research and Development page explains the technical thinking behind Quinfosys. It is not designed as a sales catalogue. It functions as a structured research library where visitors can scan core areas and expand sections for deeper detail.

Main sections:
1. Research Areas
Describes the scientific and technical directions studied by Quinfosys, including photonic quantum computing, quantum hardware, quantum communication, networks, sensors, algorithms, security, and theory.

2. Technologies
Technologies are nested inside Research and Development rather than treated as a separate navigation page. This section includes quantum computers, quantum hardware, quantum communication, quantum networks, quantum sensors, and quantum AI robotics.

3. Research Projects
Presents applied work such as quantum error correction, quantum machine learning, communication networks, sensing platforms, simulation tools, internal prototypes, and future project notes.

4. Collaborations
Frames academic, institutional, government, laboratory, and industry collaboration pathways.

Design direction:
The page uses the homepage inspired blue and dark blue shaded gray visual language. It intentionally avoids heavy gradients and uses premium editorial spacing, clean negative space, and content focused expandable sections.

Key behavior:
All expandable research sections are closed by default.
The Technologies subsection appears inside the expanded Technologies section.
The page is responsive for mobile, tablet, desktop, and wide screens.
Visible page copy avoids dash style punctuation.
