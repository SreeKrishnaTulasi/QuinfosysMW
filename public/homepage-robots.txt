# Quinfosys Homepage AI Context / Robots Note

Page: /
Purpose: The homepage is a concise gateway into Quinfosys products, solutions, services, research & development, and resources. It should not dump all legacy website text. It should open doors into subpages where detailed expandable sections live.

Design rules:
- Keep the black/white sticky-screen redesign language.
- Keep the animated quantum wave aligned to the hero heading area.
- Keep the navbar white because the logo text is black.
- Keep navbar links direct. Do not use navbar dropdowns.
- Keep Technologies nested under Research & Development.
- Keep the footer white and concise.
- Keep homepage content brief; use page-level accordions for deeper content.

Homepage section map:
1. Hero: Entangle with Quantum, animated wave, product/solution CTAs.
2. Quantum Product Portfolio: QuCPL, QCS Cloud, QWS APIs.
3. Quantum Solutions Portfolio: enterprise strategy, optimization, analytics, security, cloud migration, innovation framework.
4. Services: consulting, implementation, support, training.
5. Research & Development: research areas, technologies, projects, collaborations.
6. Resources: QisCode, documentation, white papers, case studies.
7. Concise CTA/footer.

Performance rules:
- No heavy images added to the homepage.
- No video backgrounds.
- No new animation libraries.
- SVG/CSS animation only.
- Motion is reduced when the browser requests reduced motion.
